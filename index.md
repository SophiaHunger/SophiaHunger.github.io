Thank you for visiting my homepage.

I am Junior Professor (W1, with tenure track to W2) of Computational Social Science at the University of Bremen, based at the Institute for Sociology and [SOCIUM – Research Center on Inequality and Social Policy](https://www.socium.uni-bremen.de/home/en/?), and a faculty member of the [Bremen International Graduate School of Social Sciences (BIGSSS)](https://www.bigsss-bremen.de/). Previously, I was a post-doctoral research fellow at the [Center for Civil Society Research](https://www.wzb.eu/en/research/trans-sectoral-research/center-for-civil-society-research) at the [WZB Berlin Social Science Center](https://www.wzb.eu/en), where I remain affiliated as a guest researcher. I received my doctorate from the [European University Institute](https://www.eui.eu/) in 2020.

My research focuses on protest movements, political engagement, party competition, political communication, democracy, and policy conflict, combined with applied quantitative methods — particularly quantitative text analysis, automated event extraction, and, increasingly, the use of AI and large language models in political research. I currently co-lead several large collaborative projects, including [ProTest](https://www.protestproject.eu/), a Horizon Europe-funded study of pro- and anti-democratic protest dynamics in consolidated, new, and developing European democracies, and two subprojects within the DFG-funded [Collaborative Research Center 1342 "Global Dynamics of Social Policy,"](https://www.socialpolicydynamics.de) on the political consequences of welfare state design and on contentious policy episodes. I am furthermore interested in developing new methods to measure positions, polarization, and resonance in political communication and public debate, and in studying how AI agents and large language models shape political information-seeking and democratic attitudes.
